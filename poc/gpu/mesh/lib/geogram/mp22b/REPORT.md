Tags `rayvtx=` / `rayplane=` count, exactly, +z rays through another body's contact vertex and those lying in a vertical facet plane: in the ax*, rayabove, twosided, star3 and path4 trees 53-93% of pairs have rayvtx > 0 (axvv 37 of 70, axstar3 44 of 50) (rayabove 80 of 80, twosided 50 of 50; rayplane > 0 in 73 and 46); octvert/octoct have rayvtx but no vertical planes (octahedra have none). Interpretation note: "two components on one side of the same contact vertex" is implemented as two free bodies below the contact vertex (strictly separated); two same-operand bodies sharing the vertex would be a pinched, invalid input.
